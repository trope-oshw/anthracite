# Trademark

The design files in this repository are open source under the licences listed
in the [README](README.md#licensing). **The Trope and 山本回路設計 brand
names, the Trope logo, and the product name "Anthracite" are not.** They are
trademarks of 山本回路設計, and none of those licences grants you their use.
See also section 8.2 of the CERN-OHL-S v2.

## What you may do

You are free to build, modify, and sell hardware derived from these files.
When you do, please give it your own name and your own branding. A modified
version could be called "Foobar Audio Charcoal Fuzz".

You may state truthfully that your product is *based on the Anthracite design
by Trope*.

## What you may not do

- Call your product "Anthracite" or "Trope Anthracite".
- Use the Trope logo.
- Use a name so close to Trope or Anthracite that a buyer could confuse the
  two.

## Branding in the published files

Trope removed all Trope branding from the published files, both the panel
artwork and the circuit-board silkscreen, so hardware made from these files
carries no Trope marks by default. Do not put Trope or Anthracite on the
enclosure or boards of hardware you sell as a product or brand name. The
source URL and a "based on" statement as described above are fine.

Instead of a product name, the top and bottom boards carry the board version
(`v1.1`; `V1.0` in the v1.0 files), the licence (CERN-OHL-S-2.0) and where the
source lives (`github.com/trope-oshw/anthracite`).

Boards in units built by Trope carry the product name on the silkscreen
(`Anthracite V1.0`, `Anthracite v1.1`), which the published files do not.

## Asking

Questions, or a request to use the marks: trope.oshw@gmail.com. Ask first; the
answer is almost always yes.
